How it works ?!

Learn it, choose a goal, put money in.

Most financial literacy stops at the worksheet. This one carries through to a student’s own account, which is where the habit actually forms.

Lessons

1 to 5

They learn how money behaves

Where money comes from, how behaviour drives spending, how to read your own position, and how to plan, budget and save.

6 onward

They pick a goal worth saving for

Each student names something they want, prices it, and works out how long it takes at a pace they can hold.

9 onward

They fund it in a real account

Deposits go into an account opened with our banking partner, with parent consent. The goal fills up in front of them.

In your school

What it really takes to run it

01 Timetable

One period a week, or a block in advisory or life skills. Schools also run it as an after school club. We will map the 14 lessons to whatever slot you have.

02 Cost to the school

None this academic year. The programme is funded by our banking partner, so schools in the first cohort run it without a licence fee.

03 Teacher load

Light. Lessons run and mark themselves inside the platform. The teacher opens the session, runs one short discussion, and checks the class dashboard. No finance background needed.

04 Data and safeguarding

We operate under Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law 151 of 2020. Yabi is the data controller, Edulga the processor. No student opens an account without written parent consent, and no student data is sold.

05 Platform

Students work in a Yabi Junior space built with our partner Edulga. It adapts pace and difficulty to each student, gives them an AI learning companion, and shows teachers progress at a glance.

06 Getting started

A 30 minute call, then a walkthrough for your leadership team. Schools joining for the 2026 / 27 year usually go from first call to first lesson in three to four weeks.
